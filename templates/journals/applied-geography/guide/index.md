# Applied Geography — Guide for Authors

Local, offline copy of the journal's Guide for Authors (ISSN 0143-6228,
Elsevier), downloaded on 2026-11-01. The publisher's page sits behind a
captcha, so this is the only copy that survives. Provenance is in `SOURCES.md`;
pages this guide links to but does not contain are in `external.md`.

57 sections: 49 files here and 8 that are byte-identical to the Elsevier copies
already in `_shared/elsevier/`, linked below instead of duplicated. Read the one
that owns the question; the whole guide costs more context than any single
answer is worth.

**The sections are the guide verbatim**, cut on the publisher's own headings.
Nothing is summarised: these are the limits an editor screens on.

**Three things that decide a first submission here** (each line points at the
verbatim text):

- Research articles run **4,500–8,000 words, counting captions, tables and
  references** ([Article types](sections/04-article-types.md)).
- Review is **double anonymized**: the title page is a separate file, and the
  manuscript carries no author details
  ([Double anonymized peer review](sections/27-double-anonymized-peer-review.md)).
- Any use of generative AI is declared **in the manuscript, in its own section
  before the reference list**, at first submission
  ([Declaration of generative AI use](sections/16-declaration-of-generative-ai-use.md)).

The separate *Aims and scope* page (row 3 of `SOURCES.md`) carries the same
text as section 03.
