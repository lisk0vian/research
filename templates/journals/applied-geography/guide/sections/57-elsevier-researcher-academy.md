### Resources for authors

<a id="resources-for-authors-elsevier-researcher-academy"></a>
#### Elsevier Researcher Academy

If you would like help to improve your submission or navigate the publication process, support is available via [Elsevier Researcher Academy](https://researcheracademy.elsevier.com/).

Elsevier Researcher Academy offers free e-learning modules, webinars, downloadable guides, and research writing and peer review process resources.
