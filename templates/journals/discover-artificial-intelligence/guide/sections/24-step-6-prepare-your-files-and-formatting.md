## Step 6 - Prepare your files and formatting

<a id="Step 6 - Prepare your files and formatting_"></a>
You will need to submit your manuscript in an accepted file format:

- **Word document** with figures and tables placed in the body of the text where they are referenced. For equations use the equation editor or MathType. If submitting a Data note, you **must** use the [Data note template](https://media.springer.com/full/springer-instructions-for-authors-assets/docx/Data-note-template-Discover.docx).

- **LaTeX document** with figures and tables compressed into a ZIP format. We will compile these into a PDF for peer review. We recommend using [Springer Nature’s LaTeX template](https://www.springernature.com/gp/authors/campaigns/latex-author-support).
