# Q1 review of the Colab run on current data — c19-2026

- **Run reviewed:** Colab, 2026-10-30 (RENIPRESS October 2026 + census 2025), 14 of 14 stages closed, `errors.log` empty.
- **Reviewer role:** Applied Geography referee (simulated), judging results and code against `decision-0001-contribution.md`.
- **Previous review:** `q1-review-2026-10-30.md` (before the data adaptation).

## Verdict

**Not ready to write the paper.** The run validates the data layer. It does not validate the science: the
stages still implement the old classifier design that Decision 0001 dropped, and several of its results are
wrong or meaningless. A referee would reject on the INVS alone.

## What was done well

- **Data layer.** Current sources, links and hashes (`data/SOURCES.md`), a machine-readable dictionary, census totals that reconcile with INEI's national row, 2 facilities excluded for lack of census (down from 38), unknown categories abort the ETL.
- **Reproducibility.** Cached state keyed on data and code, per-stage fingerprints, 14/14 stages with an empty error log, 18 fast tests, stage 00 verifies hashes and counts (`verify.txt` matches the registered values).
- **Honesty already in the code.** The circularity diagnostic, the "not external validation" notes, the district-grouped split that removed leakage.

## Findings (evidence in `outputs/`, code references in `experiments/`)

### Critical

1. **The INVS is inverted.** District-level Spearman of INVS with population is +0.82, with share 60+ −0.82, with share disability −0.82 and with distance to a hospital −0.51. Lima (0.905), Callao and Madre de Dios rank as the most vulnerable; Huancavelica (0.724), Moquegua and Puno as the least. PC1 measures urban versus rural, and its sign is arbitrary (`03_build_invs.py:46`, no orientation step): PCA vs FA Spearman is −0.88 and the "ablation" prints deltas of 1.91 and 1.63 (`:109-110`). KMO is 0.53, below the script's own 0.6 threshold, and PC1 explains 32 %.
2. **The target is not a health need.** 74 % of the 1,810 positives come only from `spatial_anomaly` (a geometric outlier of facility coordinates). The Sierra has 0 % anomalies, hence 2.6 % prevalence; the Selva 19.6 %. Any "regional inequity" is produced by the label.
3. **The classifier has no stable signal.** Internal AUC fell from 0.837 to 0.636 (CI 0.612–0.660) after the data and category update. Models disagree wildly on the same split: Extra Trees 0.746, XGBoost 0.605, Hist GB 0.533, Logistic 0.502, CatBoost default 0.500, Random Forest 0.448. Single-feature AUCs are 0.37–0.66. Inner CV reached 0.81 while the test gave 0.64, because one `GroupShuffleSplit` (`05_train_classifier.py:42`) left 4.5 % positives in train and 16.7 % in test. Removing a feature changes AUC by −0.09 to +0.23.
4. **The automatic verdict is wrong.** `08_test_geographic_transfer.py:97-111` prints "gap small, the model generalizes reasonably well" because it subtracts the minimum of two means. Real values: LORO in the Selva 0.33, LODO in Loreto 0.07 and Tacna 0.12, and LODO (0.744) above internal (0.636), a negative gap that signals a split lottery, not generalization.
5. **Conformal prediction does not exist in this run.** MAPIE was not importable on Colab, so `11_conformal_sota.py:48-53` silently used a Wald interval (width 0.0057) and the stage still closed as "ok". `colab.yaml` lists `mapie`; the import failed anyway.
6. **The unit of analysis is still wrong.** 27,015 facilities, 1,890 districts; target and predictors are constant within each district; Lima holds 36 % of rows. The bootstrap resamples facilities (`06_evaluate_classifier.py:25`), so its intervals are too narrow.
7. **RQ1 is not addressed.** The distance is straight-line, facility to hospital, summarised as a district median (median 0.7 km), not people to hospital and not travel time. 26 % of active facilities have no coordinates, concentrated where hospitals are (Callao 45 %, Lima 40 %, Tacna 41 % missing), and 60 of 685 complex hospitals are unlocated. Population is not located at all.

### Major

8. **Economic analysis is unsupported.** Benefit is USD 120 per person per year with no source; the main table totals USD 124.4 M while the sensitivity table totals USD 280 M for the same 35 districts (different unit costs); the "direction is stable" statement is trivial because the ratio is far below 1 in every scenario (0.03–0.11); the what-if scenario "+15 % population" gives Δ = 0.0000 because min-max scaling cancels a uniform change.
9. **Sensitivity and "causal" analyses are circular.** Sobol puts S1 = 0.71 on `share_60_plus`, which correlates 0.96 with an input of the target; the GLM regresses the target on its own inputs.
10. **Stale documentation in the outputs.** The missing-data table of stage 03 (`03_build_invs.py:316-331`) still says "fillna(0) after the census merge" and "fillna(1) via NIVEL_MAP". The ETL now drops facilities without census and uses level 0. This was left by the last adaptation and should be fixed.
11. **Spatial statistics unchanged.** Moran's I (0.257) on a 1,500-facility sample with a 1.5-degree threshold (about 165 km), computed on a district-constant variable; Getis p-values equal LISA p-values.
12. **Region by department** with an unmapped default to Sierra; unchanged.
13. **Mislabels.** "Hold-out interno (80/20 estratificado)" is not stratified; the log still calls the target "validado exteriormente"; `metricas_globales.csv` reports 3.2 s and 0 MB.

### Minor

14. Two `errors.log` files in the Drive logs folder (one is a Google Doc conversion).
15. The README of the DOI package keeps "v21 Q1 ULTRA" and the old narrative.

## Prioritised changes

**P0 — before any new experiment**
1. Stop treating stages 03–13 as the paper's results. Build RQ1: district population-weighted travel time to the nearest located complex hospital (O2, O3, O4).
2. Resolve O1 (independent outcome). Without it there is no RQ3 and the paper falls back to contribution B.
3. Remove the INVS as the headline, or rebuild it: oriented components, a justified construct, equal-weight and PCA variants compared, KMO above threshold. Test orientation with a unit test.
4. Drop the `vulnerable` target and the classifier from the main text.
5. Make the district the unit of analysis everywhere (counts, bootstrap, maps).

**P1 — needed for credibility**
6. Handle the 26 % missing coordinates: geocode from address or district centroid, and report a sensitivity analysis; locate the 60 complex hospitals.
7. If any model remains: repeated spatial cross-validation (for example 5×5), report the distribution, delete the automatic verdict.
8. Make a missing dependency fail the stage (conformal, SALib) instead of silently falling back.
9. Spatial statistics on district polygons, all districts, projected CRS, FDR.
10. District-level natural region (O5).
11. Use the monthly RENIPRESS editions (April–October 2026) as a temporal robustness check on facility status and category.

**P2 — hygiene**
12. Fix the stale tables and labels (items 10, 13), the duplicate `errors.log`, the DOI README.
13. Defer the economic analysis until RQ1–RQ3 exist; source every cost and benefit assumption.
