#### Permission for copyrighted works

If excerpts from other copyrighted works are included in your article, you must obtain written permission from the copyright owners and credit the source(s) within your article using Elsevier's[permission request and license form](https://www.elsevier.com/__data/assets/word_doc/0007/98656/Permission-Request-Form.docx).
