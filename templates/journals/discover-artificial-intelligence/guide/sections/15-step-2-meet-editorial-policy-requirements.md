## Step 2 - Meet editorial policy requirements

<a id="Step 2 - Meet editorial policy requirements_"></a>
First, check that your research is in line with the [editorial policies of the Discover Series](https://link.springer.com/brands/discover/policies).

You will need to include some policy statements in your manuscript and agree to others in Snapp, our manuscript tracking system.
