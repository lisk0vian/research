#### Publishing agreement

Authors are asked to complete a publishing agreement after acceptance. The corresponding author receives a link to the online agreement by email.

Elsevier's [policies related to copyright](https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies/copyright) explain more about our copyright policies, and additional rights for subscription and open access articles for your and your employer/institution.
