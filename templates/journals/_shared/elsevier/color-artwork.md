#### Color artwork

If you submit usable color figures with your accepted article, we will ensure that they appear in color online. Please ensure that color images are accessible to all, including those with impaired color vision. Learn more about [color and web accessibility](https://www.w3.org/WAI/perspective-videos/contrast/).
