##### Author contributions: CRediT

Corresponding authors are required to acknowledge co-author contributions using [CRediT (Contributor Roles Taxonomy)](https://credit.niso.org/) roles:

- Conceptualization

- Data curation

- Formal analysis

- Funding acquisition

- Investigation

- Methodology

- Project administration

- Resources

- Software

- Supervision

- Validation

- Visualization

- Writing – original draft

- Writing – review and editing

Not all CRediT roles will apply to every manuscript and some authors may contribute through multiple roles.

We advise you to read [more about CRediT and view an example of a CRediT author statement](https://www.elsevier.com/researcher/author/policies-and-guidelines/credit-author-statement).
