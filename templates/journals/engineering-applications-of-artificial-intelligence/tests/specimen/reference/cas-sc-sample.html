Cross-references In electronic publications, articles may be internally hyperlinked. Hyperlinks are generated from proper cross- references in the article. For example, the words Fig. 1 will never be more than simple text, whereas the proper cross- reference \ref{tiger} may be turned into a hyperlink to the figure itself: Fig. 1. In the same way, the words Ref. [1] will fail to turn into a hyperlink; the proper cross-reference is \cite{Knuth96}. Cross-referencing is possible in L A TEX for sections, subsections, formulae, figures, tables, and literature references. 9. The so-called Harvard or author-year style of referencing is enabled by the L A TEX package natbib.
