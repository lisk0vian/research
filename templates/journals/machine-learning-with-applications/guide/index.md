# Machine Learning with Applications — Guide for Authors

Local, offline copy of the journal's Guide for Authors (ISSN 2666-8270,
Elsevier). The publisher's page sits behind a captcha, so this is the only copy
that survives. Provenance is in `SOURCES.md`; pages this guide links to but does
not contain are in `external.md`.

58 sections, 960 lines. Read the one that owns the question — the whole guide
costs more context than any single answer is worth.

## Notes

- Bold entries are the ones a submission is most often sent back for.
- The publisher's grouping headings (*About the journal*, *Ethics and policies*,
  *Writing and formatting*, *Submitting your manuscript*, *After receiving a final
  decision*, *Resources for authors*, *Getting help and support*) are the headings
  above, not files: they carry no text of their own.
- The page's own table of contents is not reproduced. This index replaces it and,
  unlike it, resolves offline.
- Every link to a page outside the guide keeps its URL and is listed in
  `external.md` as `NOT MIRRORED`. That is the honest state: the text is not in the
  bank and the URL is where to get it. None of those pages states a rule that the
  guide does not already state.
