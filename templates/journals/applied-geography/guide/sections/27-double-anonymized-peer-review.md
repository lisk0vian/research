#### Double anonymized peer review

This journal follows a double anonymized review process, meaning author identities are concealed from reviewers and vice versa.

The title page (including author details) and anonymized manuscript (excluding author details) need to be submitted as separate files. In addition to the items mentioned above, the title page should also include:

- Acknowledgments. Include any individuals who provided you with help during your research, such as help with language, writing, or proofreading in the acknowledgments section. Include acknowledgments only in the title page, do not add them as a footnote to your title, or anywhere else in your article.

- Declaration of competing interests (when a separate declaration of interest file is not submitted).

- Corresponding author address: full address is required along with the email address.

The anonymized manuscript should contain the main body of your paper, including references and tables. It is important that your manuscript and any supplementary materials do not contain any identifying information such as author names or affiliations, or acknowledgments.

Read more about [peer review](https://www.elsevier.com/reviewer/what-is-peer-review#2--types-of-peer-review).
