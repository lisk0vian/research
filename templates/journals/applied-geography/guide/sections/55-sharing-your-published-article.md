#### Sharing your published article

To share your article on social media or by email, we send the corresponding author the Digital Object Identifier (DOI) link. For subscription articles we also send a customized [Share Link](https://www.elsevier.com/researcher/author/submit-your-paper/sharing-and-promoting-your-article/share-link) offering 50 days of free access.
