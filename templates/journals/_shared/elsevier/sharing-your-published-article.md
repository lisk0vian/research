#### Sharing your published article

To share your article on social media or by email, we send the corresponding author the Digital Object Identifier (DOI) link, and for subscription articles a customized [Share Link](https://www.elsevier.com/researcher/author/submit-your-paper/sharing-and-promoting-your-article/share-link) offering 50 days of free access as well.
