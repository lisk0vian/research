#### Language and editing services

We recommend that you write in American or British English but not a combination of both.

If you feel your manuscript requires editing to eliminate possible grammar or spelling errors, and conform to correct scientific English, you can use the [English Language Editing service](https://webshop.elsevier.com/language-editing-services/language-editing) provided by Elsevier’s Author Services.
