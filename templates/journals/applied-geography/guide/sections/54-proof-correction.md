#### Proof correction

To ensure a fast publication process, we ask you to provide proof corrections within two days.

Corresponding authors will receive an email with a link to our online proofing system. There you can edit text, comment on figures and tables, and answer questions raised by our copy editor. This email will include instructions and available alternative proofing methods.

Alt text for images and captions or audio descriptions for videos may have been added to improve accessibility. These have been independently generated with the aid of AI. Please check them in your proofs. You can find more information in this [alt text guide](https://www.elsevier.support/publishing/news/journal-author-guide-to-alt-text-for-images) and Elsevier's [page about improving accessibility](https://www.elsevier.com/about/accessibility).

The purpose of the proof is to check the typesetting, editing, completeness, and correctness of your article text, tables, and figures. Significant changes to your article at the proofing stage will only be considered with approval of the journal editor.
