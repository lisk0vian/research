#### Registered Report

Registered Reports are a publication format in which the research question and the quality of methodology are peer reviewed before the data are collected and analysed.
[See details of the submission process for Registered Reports.](https://media.springer.com/full/springer-instructions-for-authors-assets/pdf/Registered-report-guidelines-Discover.pdf)
