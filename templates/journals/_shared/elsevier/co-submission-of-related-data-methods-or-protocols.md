#### Co-submission of related data, methods or protocols

You are encouraged to publish a description of your research data, methods, or protocols related to your regular article as a co-submission article in [Data in Brief](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/data-in-brief) or [MethodsX](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/methodsx).

This co-submission can be submitted at the same time as your regular manuscript submission to this journal. If both your regular and co-submitted manuscripts are accepted for publication, they will be linked together on ScienceDirect, thereby promoting research reproducibility, interoperability, and open science.

- Prepare a separate manuscript describing your research data, methods, or protocols using the mandatory templates from [Data in Brief](https://legacyfileshare.elsevier.com/promis_misc/data-in-brief-article-template.docx) or [MethodsX](https://legacyfileshare.elsevier.com/promis_misc/MethodsX-Method-Article-Template.docx).

- Submit this co-submission alongside your regular manuscript as a separate submission item; on the ‘Attach files’ page in Editorial Manager, after which it will be automatically forwarded to the *Data in Brief* or *MethodsX *and assigned an editor.

- For associated Article Processing Charge (APC) details, please visit:

- [MethodsX](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/methodsx/publish/open-access-options)

- [Data in Brief](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/data-in-brief/publish/open-access-options)

Please note: Due to the automated process of submitting to *Data in Brief* or *MethodsX*, only after acceptance will you be presented with a personalized OA Article Publishing Charge based on your individual context (your country, institutional affiliation, and any society membership for example).

Please visit the [co-submission Researcher Support page](https://service.elsevier.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/38509/c/10528/supporthub/publishing/track/1POZUAo0Dv8E~YnLGiAa~yKg54Uqty75Mv_d~zj~PP9U/) for more information on how to submit a co-submission.
