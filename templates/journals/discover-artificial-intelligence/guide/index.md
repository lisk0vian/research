# Discover Artificial Intelligence — Submission Guidelines

Local, offline copy of the journal's submission guidelines (ISSN 2731-0809,
Springer Nature). The publisher's page sits behind a bot check, so this is the
only copy that survives. Provenance is in `SOURCES.md`; pages it links to but
does not contain are in `external.md`.

36 sections in 42 files. Read the one that owns the question — the whole guide
costs more context than any single answer is worth.

**The sections are the guidelines verbatim**, cut on the page's own headings —
which here are *Steps*, so the map below follows the submission sequence rather
than a topic list. Nothing is summarised.

## Two things that catch people out

- **Artwork is the most common reason a paper is returned before peer review.**
  The page says so in its own words: if reviewers cannot see the figures, they
  cannot judge the work. The DPI figures — 1200 for line art, 300 for halftone,
  600 for combination art — are in `sections/32-artwork/resolution.md`.
- **`14-review.md` and `19-peer-review-process.md` are different things.**
  *Review* is one of the ten accepted **article types**; the **peer review
  process** is how every submission is handled. They look alike and are not.

## Notes

- **Bold entries** are the ones a submission is most often sent back for.
- **Ten article types**, one file each in Step 1. *Matters Arising*, *Data Note*
  and *Registered Report* each have their own template linked from
  `external.md` — those pages are not in the bank.
- **`32-artwork/` is a folder**, not one 80-line file: placement, lettering and
  numbering, captions, resolution, and audio/video each answer a different
  question.
- The journal is **Springer Nature**, not Elsevier. Its shared pages are filed
  under `_shared/springer-nature/`, never with the Elsevier ones.
- The page's own table of contents and site navigation are not reproduced. This
  index replaces them and, unlike them, resolves offline.
- Every link to a page outside the guidelines keeps its URL and is listed in
  `external.md` as `NOT MIRRORED`: the text is not in the bank and the URL is
  where to get it.
