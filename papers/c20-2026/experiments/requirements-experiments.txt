# quietly wrong when Colab's image moves under it, with nothing to signal that
# the pin no longer matches what runs. What actually ran is recorded instead, in

# and `lxml` is the cautionary case (it compiles from source on 3.14 because no
# cp314 wheel exists for the version that resolves there).
#

# Colab runs.
