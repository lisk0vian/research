#### Language and editing services

We recommend that you write in American or British English but not a combination of both.

If you feel the English language in your manuscript requires editing to eliminate possible grammatical or spelling errors and to conform to correct scientific English, you may wish to use the [English Language Editing service](https://webshop.elsevier.com/language-editing-services/language-editing) provided by Elsevier’s Author Services.
