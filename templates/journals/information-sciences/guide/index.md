# Information Sciences — Guide for Authors

Local, offline copy of the journal's Guide for Authors (ISSN 0020-0255, Elsevier).
The publisher's page sits behind a captcha, so this is the only copy that
survives. Provenance is in `SOURCES.md`; pages this guide links to but does not
contain are in `external.md`.

55 sections in 65 files, 941 lines. Read the one that owns the question — the
whole guide costs more context than any single answer is worth.

**The sections are the guide verbatim**, cut on the publisher's own headings.
Nothing is summarised: these are the limits an editor screens on.

## The guide contradicts itself about the abstract length

Read both before trusting either:

| Section | Says |
|---|---|
| `02-article-types.md` | *"Contributions should contain an Abstract (of up to **200** words)"* |
| `25-abstract.md` | *"a concise and factual abstract which does not exceed **250** words"* |

Both sentences are in the publisher's page, verbatim. The 250 is Elsevier's
generic boilerplate; the 200 is this journal's own limit, stated in Article
types. **Use 200.** The 200 is the harder constraint, it is the journal-specific
one, and it is what the submission system will be measured against — a 240-word
abstract satisfies the sentence a reader finds first and fails the one that
matters.

## Notes

- **Bold entries** are the ones a submission is most often sent back for.
- **Review is single anonymized here** (unlike Engineering Applications of
  Artificial Intelligence), so the title page is still required but the manuscript
  is not blinded. See `03-peer-review.md` and `24-title-page.md`.
- **Two sections are folders**, not one long file: `41-article-structure/` (the
  skeleton, with CRediT and Vitae on their own) and `42-references/` (the
  reference rules per source type). Each has its own index.
- **Vitae are required** — a 100-word biography and a passport-type photo for
  each author, in an editable format. Easy to miss; it is in `41-article-structure/vitae.md`.
- **Research data is Option C**: deposit in a repository and cite it, or state
  why you cannot. A data statement is required at submission.
- The publisher's grouping headings (*About the journal*, *Ethics and policies*,
  *Writing and formatting*, *Submitting your manuscript*, *After receiving a final
  decision*, *Resources for authors*, *Getting help and support*) are the headings
  below, not files: they carry no text of their own.
- The page's own table of contents is not reproduced. This index replaces it and,
  unlike it, resolves offline.
- Every link to a page outside the guide keeps its URL and is listed in
  `external.md` as `NOT MIRRORED`: the text is not in the bank and the URL is
  where to get it.
- One link was rewritten: the article-withdrawal policy pointed at Elsevier's
  staging server, so it points at production.
